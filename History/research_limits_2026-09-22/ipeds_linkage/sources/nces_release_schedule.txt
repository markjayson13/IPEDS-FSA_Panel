Data Release Schedule
Data are submitted at the aggregated-level from postsecondary institutions and do not have student-level information. Institutions submit data through 12 interrelated survey components about general higher education topics for 3 reporting periods.
Click for more information on
IPEDS methodology.
Fall Surveys
Institutional Characteristics (IC), Completions (C), 12-month Enrollment (E12)
Winter Surveys
Admissions (ADM), Graduation Rates (GR), Student Financial Aid (SFA), Outcome Measures (OM), Cost (CST)
Spring Surveys
Fall Enrollment (EF), Finance (F), Human Resources (HR), Academic Libraries (AL)
