# Annual IPEDS documentation review

The review reads **53 distinct official dictionary vintages**: all 47 previously cached HD/FLAGS dictionaries plus six retrieved from the modern delivery endpoint (HD2023–HD2025 and FLAGS2023–FLAGS2025). There are 49 selected annual dictionaries covering HD1999–HD2025 and FLAGS2004–FLAGS2025; four earlier 2023/2024 dictionaries remain in the audit as superseded vintages. This is an annual review of definitions and coded values, not an assumption that the current dictionary applies to every year.

The [annual coverage ledger](evidence/ipeds_annual_dictionary_review.csv) identifies every file, year, component, source URL, SHA-256, archive member, field coverage and release introduction. The [field evidence](evidence/ipeds_annual_dictionary_evidence.json) preserves **916 relevant annual field entries**, including OPEID/OPEFLAG, activity, sector/universe, closure/merger and component parent/child definitions. Those government-source definitions are evidence; their frequency tables contain codes observed in that file, not necessarily every code permitted by the long description.

## Findings that change linkage and institution counts

| Field or rule | Verified meaning | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full OPEID | A six-digit eligibility identifier plus a two-digit suffix for branches, additional locations or related entities. | Preserve all eight digits, including leading zeroes. A full-ID numeric value must be padded on the left; six-digit root conversion is a separate operation. [HD2004 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2004_Dict.zip). |
| `ACT=M` | Closed in the current collection year, but active with reportable data from the prior academic year. | Do not erase an exact annual institution identity or treat all historical aid as invalid. Flag closure and review the measure's reference period. [FA2000HD dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/FA2000HD_Dict.zip), [HD2024 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2024_Dict.zip). |
| `ACT=G` | A perfect child campus whose data are reported with its main campus; appears in the dictionaries from 2014. | It is a reporting-unit relationship, not a generic inactive institution. Retain the identity and component/scope warning. [HD2014 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2014_Dict.zip). |
| `ACT=I` in 2006 | Inactive because of hurricane-related problems. | A historical disruption code omitted from a simplistic closure-only list; retain annual context. [HD2006 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2006_Dict.zip). |
| `CYACTIVE=1` | Includes current-year closures with prior-year data, as well as open, new and restored institutions. | It describes the reporting universe; it does not prove that the school was open throughout the FSA award year. [HD2003 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2003_Dict.zip). |
| `CYACTIVE=2` / `3` | Potential adds/restores versus closed, combined or out-of-scope records in the later coding scheme. | Preserve the distinction. Unknown or undocumented codes do not become active by default. [HD2010 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2010_Dict.zip). |
| `OPEFLAG=2` | A branch of a participating institution; the earlier definitions also mention system offices. | It is not equivalent to nonparticipation. Independently check administrative-office and reporting scope. [IC99_HD dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC99_HD_Dict.zip), [HD2003 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2003_Dict.zip). |
| `OPEFLAG=4` / `8` | Historically variable; later files use them for new participants during particular collection periods. | A constant `OPEFLAG==1` rule is a conservative sample restriction, not a definition of all Title IV institutions or valid matches. See the annual distinctions below. |
| `SECTOR=0` | Administrative unit only. | Separate offices from educational institutions when counting a study population, without confusing an office identity with a failed identifier match. `SECTOR=99` in some years is unknown sector, not an administrative office. [HD2003 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2003_Dict.zip). |
| `PSET4FLG` | A derived IPEDS publication/universe classification. | Use the actual year's published classification for a specified benchmark; do not reconstruct it from a single participation flag. [HD2024 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2024_Dict.zip). |
| `PRCH_*` / `IDX_*` | A relationship for the component named in the suffix. | Finance, SFA, enrollment, completions, libraries and other reporting groups are not interchangeable. [FLAGS2024 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/FLAGS2024_Dict.zip). |
| `NEWID`, `DEATHYR`, `CLOSEDAT` | Destination UNITID for merged data; year deleted from IPEDS; actual closure date. | These are distinct concepts. A deletion year is not automatically a merger/closure effective date, and a positive merger destination is not authorization to move every historical aid value. [HD2024 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2024_Dict.zip). |

## Eligibility codes must be interpreted annually

The extracted definitions show the following changes and inconsistencies:

| Directory year | `OPEFLAG=4` documentation | Other material details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1999 | Title IV eligible but outside IPEDS scope. | Codes 1/2 describe eligible institutions and eligible-main branches/system offices; 3 is deferment only. |
| 2000 | New participants eligible during the fall collection period. | This differs from the 1999 definition. |
| 2001 | The long description repeats the older out-of-scope meaning. Code 4 is absent from the observed frequency rows. | This source inconsistency must remain explicit; do not silently apply a blanket post-1999 recode. |
| 2002 | New participants eligible during the fall collection period. | Frequency code labels provide this meaning. |
| 2003 | New participants eligible after the IPEDS reporting universe was finalized. | Code 7 identifies institutions that became ineligible after universe finalization; the `PSET4FLG` definition includes those institutions but excludes new code-4 participants from its frozen publication universe. |
| 2004 | New participants during winter collection. | Code 8 identifies new participants during spring collection. |
| 2005–2025 | Observed code-4 labels, where present, refer to winter entrants; code 8, where present, refers to spring entrants. | Absence from the year's frequency table is not evidence of a policy change or deletion of the code. Preserve the raw code and dictionary availability. |

Code 3 denotes deferment-only participation; 5/6 distinguish nonparticipation with or without an OPEID on the file; 7 denotes participation stopping during the survey year in later directories. These differences describe eligibility or publication universes, not whether a known institution can receive a historical UNITID tag. A grant/loan-only sample may select a different universe than an IPEDS published institution count.

There is an explicit disagreement between official documentation products. The September 2025 College Scorecard institution documentation, page 6, groups `OPEFLAG=8` with 5/6/7 as non-Title-IV in its derived “Title IV Eligibility Type” classification. The annual HD dictionaries reviewed here describe code 8 as newly participating during spring collection. The reason for that discrepancy is unverified. For interpreting an annual HD record, retain the raw code and prefer its annual HD dictionary; Scorecard's derived grouping is not a universal historical recoding rule and does not justify excluding every code-8 institution. [Scorecard documentation, September 2025, page 6](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/files/InstitutionDataDocumentation.pdf), [HD2004 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2004_Dict.zip).

`PSET4FLG` adds a second important distinction. Modern descriptions state that First Look reports use codes **1 and 3** from the 2010 collection onward, whereas the earlier E.D. Tabs/First Look universe used **1**. Some service academies remain included by explicit exceptions despite deferment-only or non-Title-IV codes. Thus FSA observed-aid institutions, all HD records, IPEDS Title IV institutions, IPEDS institutions primarily offering postsecondary education, degree-granting institutions and First Look publication institutions are different denominators. A count comparison must state the selected universe and geography. [HD2024 dictionary, PSET4FLG](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2024_Dict.zip).

## Component-specific parent/child reporting

`PRCH_F` is the Finance relationship, `PRCH_SFA` Student Financial Aid, `PRCH_EF` fall enrollment, `PRCH_E12` twelve-month enrollment, and `PRCH_C` completions. Other suffixes refer to Human Resources, graduation rates, Outcome Measures, admissions, Academic Libraries and Cost. An institution can belong to different groups for different components. A positive Finance relationship must not remove an otherwise verified aid identity or authorize copying a Finance parent's aid to children.

For Finance, code 1 is a parent reporting combined data and code 2 a child reporting with a parent. Code 3 is a partial child; later definitions identify revenues/expenses reported locally while assets/liabilities are reported with the parent. Code 4 identifies all Finance data reported with a non-postsecondary entity; code 5 identifies partial Finance data reported with such an entity. Code 6 appears from 2016 and denotes a partial reporting child that also reports revenues/expenses for other campuses. `IDX_F` can therefore identify an intermediate reporting unit in a multi-level relationship, not necessarily the ultimate administrative headquarters. [HD2002 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/HD2002_Dict.zip), [FLAGS2016 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/FLAGS2016_Dict.zip).

`PRCHTP_F`, introduced in the reviewed 2016 files, further describes full systems, partial systems and partial children of non-IPEDS entities. Its code 3 specifically describes a partial child that also acts as a parent for other children. `PCF_F`/`PCFFP_F` are Finance allocation factors; the Finance documentation does not authorize applying them to FSA grants, Campus-Based allocations, loan amounts or borrower counts. [FLAGS2016 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/FLAGS2016_Dict.zip).

SFA's documented parent/child values normally distinguish 1 (parent), 2 (child), and -2 (not applicable). In particular, its parent link does not prove the scope of an FSA Campus-Based or Direct Loan reporting unit. `-2` is an explicit not-applicable code; a missing field or missing value is not the same finding. Old descriptions also contain `-9` for a form not mailed. Undocumented zero or arbitrary positive numbers must not be treated as valid known-scope `PRCH` values. Validate relationship codes separately from positive `IDX` UNITIDs, and preserve unknowns.

The coverage ledger records these observed schema boundaries:

- 1999 HD provides IC parent/child fields only; absence of Finance/SFA fields is a metadata limitation, not evidence that every institution reports alone.
- 2000–2003 embed component relationships in HD. In 2004 onward they are in FLAGS.
- The reviewed files introduce twelve-month-enrollment relationship fields in 2007, GR200 fields in 2009, Academic Libraries in 2014, Outcome Measures in 2015, Finance relationship type in 2016, admissions in 2019 and Cost in 2024. These are field-availability observations, not independently established dates when joint reporting first became possible.
- The refreshed 2024 FLAGS has the full later set of component relationships. The cached legacy 2024 file had only the early fall release's completions/twelve-month fields. Its absence of Finance/SFA was a **vintage/delivery issue**, not a permanent schema removal.
- The reviewed FLAGS2025 is still a partial release with completions and twelve-month-enrollment relationships only. Its missing Finance/SFA metadata limits those component screens; it does not invalidate HD2025 identity matches. [FLAGS2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/FLAGS2024_Dict.zip), [FLAGS2025](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/FLAGS2025_Dict.zip), [HD2025](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/HD2025_Dict.zip).

## Time, revisions and unresolved documentation ambiguities

HD's year is a directory/collection anchor. Component reference periods can concern prior academic or fiscal years. The `CYACTIVE` descriptions explicitly explain retaining a current-year closed institution to collect prior-year Finance, Completions and Student Financial Aid data. An FSA award-year-start or award-year-end HD join is an analytic convention that must be stated and compared; a single anchor cannot establish the proper reference period for every IPEDS outcome.

Some annual long descriptions visibly carry old boilerplate: later CYACTIVE definitions mention an outdated academic-year example, and several list restored `ACT=R` under both active and potential categories even though frequency labels distinguish `Q` as potential restore. FA2001HD repeats an older OPEFLAG-4 definition. These ambiguities are preserved in the evidence file rather than silently normalized into invented historical rules. Raw annual codes, actual release vintages, component relationships and source conflicts should remain reviewable.

The former `datacenter/data` endpoint returning 404 for 2025 did not mean the data were unavailable: official `complete-data-files` resources provide HD2025 and FLAGS2025. Every coverage decision therefore identifies the retrieved file hash and release introduction. The refreshed FLAGS2024 example also shows why a field disappearing in a cached workbook cannot automatically be attributed to policy.

The refreshed HD2023, FLAGS2023 and HD2024 dictionaries have different bytes from their legacy counterparts, so they were reviewed and retained separately. Their reviewed OPEID, activity, universe and parent/child definitions retain the same meanings after decoding Excel's literal `_x000D_` line-break escapes. HD2024 explicitly adds “(active)” to the observed `ACT=N` and `ACT=R` labels. Full FLAGS2023 adds nine `REV_*` fields for revised component data; its parent/child field set is unchanged. Those revision flags describe component-data revision status, not a new institution or a new OPEID. The substantive scope expansion remains FLAGS2024: 16 to 118 total documented variables, with the previously absent later-release component relationships restored. [HD2023](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/HD2023_Dict.zip), [FLAGS2023](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/FLAGS2023_Dict.zip), [HD2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/HD2024_Dict.zip).

This review supports separate flags for **identity evidence, IPEDS universe, directory activity, component reporting scope, changing identifiers and source vintage**. Combining all of them into a single match-success indicator discards valid identity matches and obscures the actual reason institutional counts differ.
